# Air troubleshooting

- US coverage only. Set coordinates and explicitly enable locationConfigured before expecting provider requests.
- Unavailable or stale readings are not a clean-air guarantee. Regional AQI is not a reading from a sensor at your address.
- Severe active alerts include a bounded on-screen headline and guidance. Consult the National Weather Service for complete official instructions.

Use the README for settings, dependencies, privacy and sources. For a bug report, include your device, MagicMirror and Node versions, the observed behavior and a minimal configuration with secrets removed. Do not post private feeds, tokens, precise home coordinates or network snapshots.
